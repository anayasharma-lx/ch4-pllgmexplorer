Area of a Parallelogram

Drag the handles to reshape the parallelogram. The dashed rectangle always has the same area — shearing doesn't change it.

The key idea: cut the triangle off the left side and move it to the right — the parallelogram becomes a rectangle with the same base and height. So Area = base × height, not base × slant side.
